# Contributing

Thank you for suggesting an entry. These rules apply to every list in this family.

## The rules

- One entry per pull request.
- One line per entry, in the format `- [Name](url) - One sentence ending in a full stop.`
- Keep each section in alphabetical order by name.
- The link goes to the thing itself (the form, the dataset, the tool, the community), not to a page about it.
- Write plain English. No marketing adjectives, and no em dashes.
- Do not add rankings or claims you cannot point to on the linked page.
- Run `npx awesome-lint` before you open the pull request. The same check runs in CI, along with a link check.

## What belongs here

An entry qualifies when it measures AI on legal work and can be checked. That means a benchmark, an evaluation dataset, a leaderboard, a grading rubric or a harness, with a stated task and a stated way of scoring it, and a reachable page for the data, the harness, the paper or the leaderboard, even where the full data is held back. The link goes to the thing itself, meaning the repository, the dataset card, the paper or the leaderboard, rather than to a press article about it.

Each benchmark gets one line. Where it has a paper, a dataset and a leaderboard, the secondary links go in the prose of that line. Say who made it and when, what it measures and how large it is, and give the licence where the page states one. Use a number only when it appears on the linked page, and leave rankings of models or vendors to the linked pages. A benchmark that is withdrawn or superseded stays on the list, with a linked note saying so.

Contract forms, playbooks and tools belong in [awesome-contract-negotiation](https://github.com/HumblePaper/awesome-contract-negotiation). General legal NLP resources belong in the related lists linked from the README.
