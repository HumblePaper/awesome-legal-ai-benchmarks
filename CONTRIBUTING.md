# Contributing

Thank you for suggesting an entry. These rules apply to every list in this family.

## The rules

- One entry per pull request.
- One line per entry, in the format `- [Name](url) - One sentence ending in a full stop.`
- Keep each section in alphabetical order by name.
- The link goes to the thing itself (the form, the dataset, the tool, the community), not to a page about it.
- Write plain English. No marketing adjectives, and no em dashes.
- Do not add rankings or claims you cannot point to on the linked page.
- Run `npx awesome-lint` before you open the pull request. The same check runs in CI, along with a link check.

## What belongs here

An entry belongs if all three are true:

- It measures AI on legal work: a benchmark, an evaluation dataset, a leaderboard, a grading rubric or a harness, with a stated task and a stated way of scoring it.
- It is public enough to be checked: the data, the harness, the paper or the leaderboard page is reachable, even where the full data is held back.
- The link is the thing itself: the repository, the dataset card, the paper or the leaderboard, not a press article about it.

Also:

- One line per benchmark. A benchmark with a paper, a dataset and a leaderboard gets one line with the secondary links in prose.
- State who made it, when, what it measures and how big it is. Give the licence where the page states one.
- Numbers only when they appear on the linked page. No rankings of models or vendors in the description.
- A benchmark that is withdrawn or superseded stays, with a linked note saying so.

Contract forms, playbooks and tools belong in [awesome-contract-negotiation](https://github.com/HumblePaper/awesome-contract-negotiation); general legal NLP resources belong in the related lists linked from the README.
